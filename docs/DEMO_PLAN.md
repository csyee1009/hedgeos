# HedgeOS 90-Second Judge Demo Plan

---

## Pitch Narrative Structure

1. **Problem (15s)**: "Crypto holders want to protect their ETH downside before major market events, but traditional options interfaces force them to navigate strikes, expiries, IV, and RFQs."
2. **Intent Entry (20s)**: "HedgeOS asks one simple question: 'What do you want to protect?' The user enters: 'Protect my 2 ETH until Friday, max downside 8%, budget 3 USDC.'"
3. **Intent Parsing & Confirmation (15s)**: "AI parses the natural language into a Typed Risk Intent with full provenance tracking. The user explicitly confirms the interpretation."
4. **Feasibility & Financial Constitution (20s)**: "The Protection Solver checks live Thetanuts OptionBook liquidity and RFQ sources. Stage 1 Hard Constraints reject candidates exceeding budget, while Stage 2 ranks viable hedges. The deterministic Financial Constitution verifies policy invariants."
5. **Execution Preview (20s)**: "HedgeOS displays the exact protection summary, cost, duration, and transparent trade-offs, with exact-intent binding verification ready for user authorization."

**Closing Statement**: "The future of DeFi options isn't asking users which instrument to buy — it's executing the outcome they want."
