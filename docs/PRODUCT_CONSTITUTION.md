# HedgeOS Product Constitution

**Working Tagline**: "Protect outcomes, not instruments."  
**Target Tracks**: Best Product Built on Thetanuts SDK & AI × Options  

---

## 1. Product Mission & Philosophy

HedgeOS transforms options trading in Web3 from an instrument-first selection paradigm into an outcome-first intent execution model.

Users should never be forced to understand options mechanics (strikes, expiries, puts/calls, Greeks, IV, RFQs, contract calldata) to protect their portfolios. Instead, users specify their financial outcome in plain language:
- What asset they hold
- How long they need protection
- Maximum downside tolerance
- Maximum protection budget

HedgeOS resolves this outcome into a policy-verified, executable option strategy powered by Thetanuts Finance.

---

## 2. Inviolable Core Product Principles

1. **Outcome-First UX**: The primary interface accepts desired financial outcomes. Options parameters are presented via progressive disclosure.
2. **Deterministic Safety Boundary**: The AI parser only *proposes* structured intents. Hard safety policies strictly *verify* constraints. Humans *authorize* transactions.
3. **No Unsubstantiated Claims**: Never promise "guaranteed risk-free return", "zero loss", or "perfect AI selection". Use rigorous financial terminology.
4. **Load-Bearing Integration**: Thetanuts Finance is the load-bearing options protocol. OptionBook limit orders and OptionFactory RFQs are directly queried and resolved.
5. **Exact-Intent Binding**: Final transaction payloads are deterministically bound to user-confirmed risk parameters prior to preview and authorization.

---

## 3. Explicit Non-Goals

- **No Multi-Chain Sprawl**: Targeting Base Mainnet (Chain ID 8453) exclusively. No Sui, Gonka, or cross-chain bridges.
- **No Unrelated Features**: No lending, prediction markets, Polymarket clones, social feeds, or yield farming aggregators.
- **No Automated Unbounded Execution**: No background automated transaction execution without explicit user authorization per action.
